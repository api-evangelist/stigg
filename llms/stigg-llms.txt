# Stigg

> Stigg is a subscription and entitlement management platform for SaaS companies. Model pricing with plans, add-ons, credits, and usage-based billing; provision customers and subscriptions; enforce feature access; render pricing tables, paywalls, and customer portals; and integrate with Stripe, Zuora, HubSpot, Salesforce, and AWS Marketplace. Available as backend SDKs (Node.js, Python, Ruby, Go, Java, .NET), frontend SDKs (React, Vue, Next.js), GraphQL API, and REST API.
