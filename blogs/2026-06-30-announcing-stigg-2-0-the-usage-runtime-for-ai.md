Stigg 2.0 introduces a real-time enforcement platform built for AI product monetization, addressing gaps in traditional billing systems that cannot make instantaneous access decisions. The release includes a rebuilt credits engine, governance controls for enterprise budgets, millisecond-latency metering, and a bring-your-own-cloud (BYOC) deployment architecture.
