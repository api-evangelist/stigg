Stigg is deepening its credit-based pricing capabilities with granular event dimensions, isolated credit pools, and advanced time-series filtering to give teams complete control over credit-based pricing. The update treats credits as internal currency requiring the same precision, safety, and auditability as financial transactions, targeting modern AI and API economy use cases.
